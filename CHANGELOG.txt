GeoStamp Evidence ID + Device QR Patch

Modified files only:
1. VerificationEngine.kt
2. MainViewModel.kt
3. EvidenceRegistryOutbox.kt

Implemented:
- One permanent user-facing identifier: Evidence ID.
- Old Verification ID remains only as a compatibility alias with exactly the same value.
- QR marker upgraded to v3.
- QR now carries public-safe device-of-capture fields:
  masked GeoStamp Device Identity, phone brand, model and capture-key fingerprint prefix.
- Every new metadata record stores evidenceId.
- Public registry files use the same Evidence ID.
- Device identity is generated before stamping so the QR and final evidence metadata refer to the same capture device.
- No extra identifier is generated at registration or verification.

Important:
The QR cannot contain the final image hash or capture signature because the QR is drawn before the final stamped image exists. Those fields remain in the sealed metadata/public registry report.

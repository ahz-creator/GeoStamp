IMPLEMENTED

• One-time Device & Evidence Integrity consent.
• No repeated user notification for routine device-identity matching.
• GeoStamp Device Identity (GDI), derived from Android-scoped identity, app signing
  certificate and phone brand/model/device environment.
• Phone manufacturer, brand and model retained in evidence metadata.
• Android Keystore ECDSA signing key generated automatically.
• StrongBox requested first where available; TEE/software fallback supported.
• Every sealed capture is signed using SHA256withECDSA.
• Capture public key, key fingerprint, signature and signed payload retained.
• Hardware-backed status/security level retained.
• Public-safe registry record includes masked device identity and signature proof.
• Internal previous capture-key fingerprint is retained silently if a key changes.
• IMEI, IMSI and SIM serial are not accessed or fabricated.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

A normal Play Store application cannot guarantee an identifier that changes only when
the physical phone changes. GDI is the strongest Play-safe continuity identity and
normally survives app updates, reboot, cache clearing and uninstall/reinstall when the
same device/user and signing certificate remain. Factory reset, Android profile change,
OS behavior or signing-key change can change it. GeoStamp records such changes silently
for internal comparison instead of making an unsupported absolute claim.

GeoStamp Phase 6 — Overlay and Workspace Rules

1. Saved-photo evidence overlay defaults to 25% image height and is hard-limited to 20–30%.
2. QR scales with the smaller overlay.
3. Live camera information has one exclusive mode: Floating Card, Bottom Strip, or Off.
4. Floating Card and Bottom Strip can no longer appear together.
5. Personal visit history requires an exact Project/Title + Reference match before location-radius matching.
6. Organization mode no longer permits manual organization changes on the camera screen.
7. Settings organization selector is read-only and prepared for verified-email sign-in assignment.
8. Site ID remains automatic from GPS/site database.
9. Manual field-engineer username setting is removed; current fallback is Guest until sign-in is connected.

PHASE 9 — PUBLIC VERIFICATION PORTAL

Changed Android files:
- VerificationEngine.kt
- MainViewModel.kt

New zero-cost web portal:
- docs/index.html
- docs/styles.css
- docs/app.js

QR codes now open a public GitHub Pages verification URL and carry compact evidence fields in a Base64-URL payload. The receiver can scan the QR using any phone camera. The portal also attempts local QR extraction from a selected image when the browser supports BarcodeDetector.

No photo is uploaded. Exact image-hash matching is intentionally not claimed until a public evidence registry exists.

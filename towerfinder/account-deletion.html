Delete Your Tower Finder Account

App: Tower Finder
Developer: AHZ Global Services
Support: ahztravellers@gmail.com

Delete inside the Android app

  1. Sign in to Tower Finder.
  2. Open Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and tap Delete Permanently.
  5. If Firebase requires a recent sign-in, log out, sign in again and repeat the deletion steps.

A successful in-app deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, the Firestore user profile and Tower Finder's device-local location, serving-cell, recent-search and cached tower data.

Request deletion without the app

Email ahztravellers@gmail.com from the registered address with the subject Tower Finder Account Deletion Request.

Include the registered email address, display name if available, and a clear request to delete the account and associated profile data.

The developer may ask for reasonable identity verification. Records required for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance will be limited to what is necessary.

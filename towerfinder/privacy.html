Tower Finder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 August 2026
Developer: AHZ Global Services
Contact: ahztravellers@gmail.com

Tower Finder provides telecom serving-site, nearby-tower, signal and route-planning features.

Information used by the app

  • Account data: Email address, display name, phone number and company details supplied during registration.
  • Location data: Precise or approximate foreground location for nearby sites, distance, direction, maps and route planning.
  • Cellular information: Operator, radio technology and available MCC, MNC, TAC/LAC, CID, PCI/PSC and signal measurements for serving-site matching.
  • Technical data: Installation identifier, app version, network requests, diagnostics, crash information and basic analytics.
  • Advertising data: Consent status and advertising-related data processed by Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform when advertising is enabled.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide tower intelligence, match serving cells, calculate nearby distance and direction, generate routes, protect the API, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and display consent-based advertising.

Service providers

Tower Finder uses Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics and App Check; Google Maps, Google Play services, Google Mobile Ads and UMP; and a secured Cloudflare Worker/D1 API.

Unknown-cell improvement records

When a serving cell is not yet mapped, the app may submit the cell identifiers, operator/radio information, signal value and location rounded to approximately three decimal places to improve future matching.

Storage and retention

A limited local cache is stored on the device for continuity. Account and operational records are retained for as long as required to provide the service, secure it, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Uninstalling removes the device-local cache but does not automatically delete the Firebase account or server-side records.

Your choices

Location and phone-state permissions can be changed in Android App Settings. Advertising consent can be reviewed through Settings → Privacy Choices whenever the option is required in the user's region.

A signed-in user can permanently delete the Firebase Authentication account, Firestore profile and device-local Tower Finder caches through Settings → Delete Account. Users may also request access, correction or deletion by contacting ahztravellers@gmail.com.

Children

Tower Finder is a professional field utility and is not directed to children.

Changes

Material updates will be reflected in this public policy and, where relevant, in the app.
